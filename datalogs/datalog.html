CONTROLS

WASD

Move Camera: Use W, A, S, D keys to navigate the camera across the battlefield.

SPACE / SHIFT

Move Up / Down: Use the SPACE bar to move the camera up and the SHIFT key to move it down.

MOUSE

Look Around: Move the mouse to change the camera's viewing direction.

CLICK

Deploy Weapon: Left-click to deploy selected weapons or interact with UI elements.

P

Pause Game: Press 'P' to pause or unpause the game, allowing you to strategize.

ESC

Release Cursor: Press 'ESC' to release the mouse cursor from game control, enabling interaction with external applications or menus.

Y

Interact (Primary): Press 'Y' to interact with primary game objects and activate their functions.

T

Interact (Secondary): Press 'T' to interact with secondary game objects or toggle specific features.

E

Interact (Contextual): Press 'E' for contextual interactions, suchs as picking up items or opening doors.
